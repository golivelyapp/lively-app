Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026. Lively — a service operated in Bangalore, India.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Lively collects when you use our mobile app and website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. If any of this is unclear, email us at golively.app@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Lively is a members-only community for discovering and joining real-world activities in Bangalore. "We", "us", and "our" refer to the operator of the Lively app; "you" and "your" refer to the person using it. Our contact is golively.app@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Basic profile — your name, date of birth, gender, city/neighbourhood, profile photo, short bio, and the activities you're interested in.
  • Identity check — a selfie taken through the app so we can match it to your profile photo. Selfies are used only for identity confirmation and are stored securely.
  • Optional profile enhancements — height, personality traits, favourite musicians/movies/dishes, social handles (Instagram, LinkedIn, X), employer/profession/relationship status — all optional and editable from your profile.
  • Host verification — if you apply to host events, we additionally collect a government-issued ID photo, an Instagram link, and a short application. This information is retained only for as long as needed to verify you and comply with legal obligations.
  • Chat messages — the group and direct messages you send inside the app.
  • Reports — content or people you report to us, along with the report reason.

Information we collect automatically

  • Location — your neighbourhood in Bangalore, either from your phone's location (with your permission) or the area you pick manually.
  • Device data — Android version, device model, app version, crash logs, and a device push token so we can send notifications.
  • Activity data — events you RSVP to, waves you send, chats you're part of, and interactions inside the app.

Information from third parties

  • Google account — when you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile photo from Google. We never receive your Google password.
  • Apple account (if used) — similarly, we receive whatever Apple shares under your privacy choices.

3. How we use it

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To verify your identity so the community stays real.
  • To match you to relevant events based on your interests, gender, and neighbourhood.
  • To power in-app features: event browsing, RSVPs, group chats, direct messages after mutual waves, notifications.
  • To keep the community safe — including reviewing reports, moderating harassment, and where necessary suspending or removing accounts.
  • To improve the product using aggregate, non-identifying analytics.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the following categories of recipients, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Supabase (Amazon Web Services) — our primary backend and database. Hosted in a Supabase-managed AWS region.
  • Google Firebase — for push notifications, crash reporting, and analytics.
  • Google Cloud (OAuth) — for Google sign-in only.
  • Other Lively members — your name, profile photo, bio, activities, and RSVP history are visible to other verified members. If you enable "blurred profile" in Privacy settings, non-attendees see a blurred photo and "Private" instead of your name.
  • Law enforcement — where a valid legal request (court order, subpoena) compels us to disclose.

5. Your choices

  • See your data — email golively.app@gmail.com and we'll send you an export within 30 days.
  • Correct your data — most fields are editable from the You tab in the app. For anything else, email us.
  • Delete your account — from You → Settings → Log out is a sign-out, not a delete. To delete permanently, email us — we'll remove your profile within 30 days. Chat messages you sent will remain visible in group chats you were part of but will be attributed to "Deleted user" with your photo removed.
  • Notifications — you can disable push notifications in your phone's OS settings for the Lively app.
  • Location — you can revoke location permission from your phone's settings; you can still use Lively by selecting your neighbourhood manually.

6. Security

We use Supabase Row-Level Security to enforce that you can only see the data you're allowed to see. Passwords are never stored by us — Google and Apple handle authentication. Photos and IDs are stored in access-controlled Supabase Storage buckets. Traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted via HTTPS. No system is perfect; if we discover a breach that affects you, we will notify you promptly as required by law.

7. Age restriction

Lively is for people 18 years and older. If we learn we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 18 has an account, email us and we will remove it.

8. Data retention

  • Account and profile data: while your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion.
  • Selfie verification and host ID: retained for the duration of your account.
  • Chat messages: kept as long as the chat exists (event chats archive 48 hours after the event ends).
  • Reports and safety records: retained up to 3 years for the safety of the community.

9. International transfers

Our backend (Supabase, Google Firebase) may process your data in servers outside India. Where this happens, we rely on the standard contractual protections offered by these providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change and we will notify you in the app for material changes.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: golively.app@gmail.com.
