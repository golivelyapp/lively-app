What is Lively?

Lively is a members-only community for discovering and joining real-world activities in Bangalore — art jams, pub quizzes, lake walks, board game nights, and more — with verified people who live near you.

How it works

Every member is verified with a live selfie. Every host is verified with a government ID. Events are gender-balanced by default. You browse events near you, RSVP, and meet everyone in the group chat before the event even starts.
